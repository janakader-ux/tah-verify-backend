The local picture

How big the Edinburgh verification market actually is

Edinburgh has 60,520 live companies registered to its post town, and the EH postcode area holds 79,881 (Companies House Free Company Data Product, snapshot 2026-08-01). Edinburgh is also where Companies House maintains its Scottish office, which occasionally leads directors to assume that a Scottish company follows a separate verification process. It does not. The same guidance, the same accepted documents and the same deadlines apply.

Scotland's register held 275,310 companies at 31 March 2026 (Companies register activities 2025–26), and Edinburgh and Glasgow between them account for the majority of it, with Aberdeen (14,494) and Dundee (10,625) behind them (Companies House bulk data). Company profiles

Typical Edinburgh companies, and what that means for verification

Edinburgh's company population is unusually weighted towards financial and professional services, fund and investment structures, and partnerships. That matters for verification in two specific ways. First, LLP members and general partners are in scope now, while corporate directors, corporate LLP members and officers of corporate PSCs will be required to verify at a later date (GOV.UK). Firms with layered structures therefore need to separate what is due this year from what is not. Second, a person with significant control who is not also a director has a completely different deadline from a director: the first 14 days of their birth month (GOV.UK). Investment structures frequently have exactly that kind of non-director PSC.

The requirement

Who must verify, and by when

Everyone in scope is in scope in Edinburgh on exactly the same terms as anywhere else in the UK. There is no local variation, no local extension and no local exemption.

GOV.UK lists the people who must verify as a director, the equivalent of a director, which includes members, general partners and managing officers, a director of an overseas company registered in the UK, a person with significant control, and anyone registering as an Authorised Corporate Service Provider. People who file on behalf of a company, limited partnerships, corporate directors, corporate LLP members and officers of corporate PSCs will be required to verify at a later date.

Identity verification became a legal requirement on 18 November 2025. Companies House is explicit that this date is not a deadline in itself; it marks the start of a 12-month transition period that ends in November 2026, by the end of which Companies House expects to have verified 6 to 7 million individuals (Annual Report and Accounts 2025–26). Your own deadline sits inside that window and depends on your role:

  • Existing directors: by the company's next confirmation statement date.
  • New directors: before appointment, and at incorporation for a new company.
  • Directors of overseas companies registered in the UK: by the anniversary of the UK establishment's registration.
  • A PSC who is also a director: within the 14-day period starting the day after the confirmation statement date.
  • A PSC who is not a director: within the first 14 days of their birth month.

Once you have verified, Companies House issues an 11 character personal code. It is personal to you and not to your company, and if you are a director of multiple companies you only need to verify once and then use the same code for every appointment.

If you do not verify

What happens if the deadline passes

The consequences of not verifying are national and they are not administrative niceties. They apply to a Edinburgh director in the same way as to any other.

Companies House states that it is unlawful for a director to act as a director without completing identity verification, and that the offence arises under section 167M of the Companies Act. Three enforcement routes are available: criminal prosecution, referral to the Insolvency Service, and financial penalties. Prosecution may lead to criminal conviction, director disqualification and fines, and non-compliance can be annotated on the public register for anyone to see.

There is also an immediate, practical consequence that arrives long before any enforcement action. All directors need to verify before the company's next confirmation statement is filed, otherwise the filing will be rejected. The campaign guidance puts it more bluntly still: without verification you will not be able to make any filings for your company or start a new company. For most people that rejected filing, not the prospect of prosecution, is what makes this urgent.

Questions

Edinburgh verification questions

The questions we are asked most often by directors and PSCs of Edinburgh companies.

Does Companies House being in Edinburgh mean Scottish verification is different?

No. Companies House maintains a Scottish office in Edinburgh, but the identity verification standard, the accepted documents and the deadlines are UK-wide and identical.

How many companies are registered in Edinburgh?

There are 60,520 live companies registered to the Edinburgh post town and 79,881 across the EH postcode area, on the Companies House snapshot of 1 August 2026.

I am a PSC of an Edinburgh company but not a director. When is my deadline?

If you are a PSC who is not a director, your verification window is the first 14 days of your birth month. If you are a PSC who is also a director, it is the 14-day period starting the day after the confirmation statement date.

Do fund and partnership structures need to verify yet?

Individual LLP members and general partners are in scope now. Corporate directors, corporate LLP members and officers of corporate PSCs will be required to verify at a later date.

Can you verify an Edinburgh director who lives abroad?

Yes, but the fee is £119 rather than the UK rate of £49 to £125 for an applicant resident outside the UK. Our country pages set out what that involves.

The process

Three steps, start to finish

No jargon, no chasing paperwork you don't understand. Here's the whole thing.

01

Apply & review documents

Complete your details, sign the engagement letter and upload clear document photographs. We review their preliminary suitability before asking you to pay.

02

Pay & verify your identity

After your preliminary review is complete, pay your confirmed fee. Then complete a digital check via our sub-processor TrustID (about 10 minutes), or book an in-person appointment at our Bedford or London–Stratford office if you don't hold a biometric passport. See the full step-by-step guide.

Who needs this

If Companies House knows your name, this applies to you

Under the Economic Crime and Corporate Transparency Act 2023, identity verification is now mandatory for anyone with a formal relationship to a UK company or LLP.

  • Company directors

    Every director of a UK company, whether UK-resident or overseas.

  • People with significant control (PSCs)

    Individuals who own or control 25%+ of shares or voting rights.

  • LLP members

    Designated and non-designated members of a limited liability partnership.

  • Filers & general partners

    Anyone filing on behalf of a company, and general partners of limited partnerships.

Common questions

Quick answers

Is this fully remote?

Yes — no travel or appointment is needed for the digital route. The two-document route is different: original documents have to be examined in person, by appointment at our Bedford or London–Stratford office.

What does it cost?

£49, all-inclusive, for a UK-resident director verified remotely; £125 if you need an in-person appointment in Bedford or London–Stratford; £119, all-inclusive, for a foreign or overseas director verified remotely. Standard office appointments are £125 for either residency group. There are no hidden extras and no VAT.

Can someone else complete the check for me?

No — the identity check must be completed personally. It cannot be delegated to a third party.

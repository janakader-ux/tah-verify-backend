Director Personal Code — staff sign-in

Director Personal Code is the Companies House identity verification service of Tax And Accounting Hub Ltd, a Companies House Authorised Corporate Service Provider.

Director Personal Code — Applications

Online / remote route (TrustID Guest Link): as soon as Payment shows Paid, the backend automatically logs into TrustID and emails the applicant their Guest Link — no staff action needed. The “Verification” column shows the outcome (“link sent” = done). Only use “Copy details” / “Open TrustID” as a manual fallback if that column shows an error or “manual action required”.

In-person / manual route (appointment): once Payment shows Paid, call or email the applicant on the details shown to confirm the exact date/time against their preferred date and time window, at the office they selected. Remind them what to bring/post per the office notes on the application. Once verification passes, complete the Companies House / GOV.UK manual portal entry — there is no public API for that step either.

Companies House Personal Code Not Working? What to Check in 2026

Practical help16 November 2026·5 min read·By Director Personal Code

Already verified, but cannot finish your Companies House filing? First identify whether you need to recover a code, correct a record or complete identity verification. Paying for another identity check may not solve the problem.

This November 2026 checklist helps directors and people with significant control (PSCs) decide what to do next. It includes the July 2026 update on receiving personal codes by email.

1. Check the code and recover it if necessary

Your personal code has 11 characters and identifies you; it differs from a company's authentication code. Check your entry carefully.

  • Verified through GOV.UK One Login? Sign in to Companies House with the email used to verify and open Manage account. People verified after 8 July 2026 were also emailed their code.
  • Verified through an ACSP? Check the email address your agent supplied. Contact that agent if the code has not arrived or that email was wrong.

2. Investigate details that do not match

A rejected code may indicate an incorrect date of birth on the Companies House record. Correct that before providing verification details. Ask your original ACSP about incorrect verification information they supplied. Follow the official personal-code and correction guidance.

3. Check which role and company need the code

Getting verified and completing a filing are different milestones. Existing directors provide their code through the relevant confirmation statement. A director who is also a PSC must provide it separately for that role. For several companies, check each filing. See the official rules for your role.

For example, if you have finished the director filing but still see a PSC reminder, check the PSC requirement before buying another identity check. Read our guide to one code across several company roles.

4. Check your own deadline

Do not rely on a general November headline: timing depends on your role and appointment. Check the company's confirmation statement requirements and any PSC window shown on the register.

If you cannot meet a PSC deadline, the official PSC service permits a request for a 14-day extension before the deadline passes. Do not assume this extends a director's filing deadline.

When does paid ACSP verification help?

If you still need identity verification, you can use GOV.UK One Login free of charge or a registered authorised agent. In most cases, you verify once; Companies House says not to repeat verification unless instructed. Read its verification guidance.

Professional support can help you assess your documents and choose an appropriate verification route. It does not remove the need for personal identity checks or guarantee that a filing error will be resolved.

Unsure whether you need a new identity check?

Ask Director Personal Code, provided by Tax And Accounting Hub Ltd, before paying. Describe the issue and the type of document you hold. Please do not send personal codes, passport numbers, dates of birth or identity documents in a general enquiry.

Before you try again

  • Write down the exact error message without sharing your code.
  • Identify the company and whether you are acting as director, PSC or both.
  • Check the information against the official guidance.
  • Contact Companies House or your original verifying agent where a record needs correcting.
  • Only start a paid application once you understand what it will solve.

Your payment: next steps

After your payment clears, we email a confirmation containing your case reference. Return to your original application tab to check its payment status. Opening this page alone does not confirm payment; if you have paid, please do not pay again.

What happens next

  • If you chose the online ID check: TrustID, our identity verification provider, will email you a secure link to complete your check — usually within a few minutes. Open it on a phone with a camera and have your passport or driving licence to hand. If you cannot find it, please check your spam or junk folder, as it is sent by TrustID rather than by us.
  • If you chose an in-person appointment: return to your application tab to pick your office and preferred date. If you have already closed it, no problem — email us and we will arrange your slot.

Any questions, email info@taxandaccountinghub.com quoting your case reference. If you have not received a confirmation, we can check the payment against your case reference.

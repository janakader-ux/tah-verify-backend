Engagement Letter — ACSP Identity Verification (Individuals)

Tax And Accounting Hub Ltd ("we", "us")
Registered in England & Wales, Company No. 08408126
Registered office: 11 Holbeach Avenue, Shortstown, Bedford, MK42 0EG
AAT Licensed Accountant. Supervised by the Association of Accounting Technicians (AAT) for anti-money laundering purposes. Registered with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

1. Purpose and scope of this engagement

You have asked us to act as your Authorised Corporate Service Provider (ACSP) for the purpose of verifying your identity with Companies House, under the Economic Crime and Corporate Transparency Act 2023 and the Registrar's (Identity Verification by ACSPs) Rules. Our services under this engagement are:

  • Collecting your personal data as required for identity verification;
  • Facilitating a digital identity verification check (including facial matching and liveness detection) through our identity service sub-processor, TrustID, where you select the online route;
  • Independently reviewing the outcome of the TrustID check, or, where you select the in-person route, personally examining your original identity document at your appointment;
  • Submitting an Identity Verification Statement to Companies House on your behalf, confirming that we have verified your identity to the required standard;
  • Confirming the outcome to you in writing; and
  • Retaining records of the engagement as required by law.

2. Fee

Our fee for this service is £49, all-inclusive, based on the residency status and verification route you selected earlier in this application (UK-based director — remote check). Payment is due after you sign this engagement letter, before we open your identity verification link or confirm an appointment. It is collected securely online.

3. How your identity will be verified

You provide your identity documents securely through TrustID after payment, or bring the required originals to your appointment. No document upload is required on this website. Depending on the route you selected:

Online route — once your payment has been received, we will email you a secure one-time link to our identity verification partner, TrustID. Using your own smartphone or webcam, you will be guided through capturing your identity document and a short liveness/facial-matching check directly within TrustID's secure portal.

In-person route — you will attend a booked appointment at one of our offices (Bedford or London — Stratford), where a member of our staff will personally examine your original photo identity document. Bring two qualifying original documents with you; uploaded photographs do not replace the original-document check.

If you are not resident in the United Kingdom, your identity document must be government-issued (for example, a passport). All documents must be seen in their original form — we cannot accept photocopies, or photographs of copies.

4. Your responsibilities

You confirm that:

  • All information you provide to us will be true, accurate and complete;
  • You will complete the identity check personally — it cannot be completed on your behalf by a third party;
  • You will complete the identity verification check within 14 days of receiving your verification link, after which the link will expire (online route only);
  • You will provide an email address that only you can access, as Companies House will send your personal verification code directly to this address; and
  • You will notify us promptly of any change in your circumstances relevant to this engagement.

5. Data protection

Tax And Accounting Hub Ltd is the data controller for the personal data you provide. Our lawful basis for processing is the performance of a legal obligation arising under UK corporate transparency and anti-money-laundering law. Where biometric data is processed (for facial matching and fraud prevention), this is done under the substantial public interest condition of the Data Protection Act 2018.

We use TrustID as our identity verification sub-processor for digital checks. See our privacy notice for details. Companies House receives only your name, date of birth, home address, email address, and document type/reference/expiry/country of issue — it does not receive copies of your documents. Once your personal code is connected to your Companies House record, the public register will show your name, our firm name as your ACSP, our AML supervisory body, and the date your checks were completed — it will not show your documents or home address.

6. Record retention

We retain formal identity-verification records, including failed verification attempts, for 7 years from the date the engagement is completed, after which they are securely destroyed. This retention period cannot be shortened at your request, as it is required by law.

7. Liability, confidentiality and termination

Our liability under this engagement is capped at the fee you paid (£49 to £125, as applicable to your case), except where liability cannot lawfully be limited. We will keep your information confidential, save where we are required by law to make a report (for example, under money laundering regulations) — we are not permitted to notify you if such a report is made. Either party may terminate this engagement by written notice; fees for work already completed remain payable. This engagement is governed by the law of England and Wales.

8. What this engagement does not cover

This engagement does not include: incorporating a company, appointing directors, other Companies House filings, connecting your personal verification code to your Companies House record (you must do this yourself once you receive your code), verifying the identity of any other individual (a separate engagement is required for each person), Register of Overseas Entities verification, or tax, legal or immigration advice.

9. Complaints

If you are unhappy with our service, please contact Kader Ameen, Director, at Kader@taxandaccountinghub.com. We will acknowledge your complaint within 5 working days and provide a full response within 20 working days. If you remain unsatisfied, you may escalate your complaint to the AAT.

What happens now

  • Your case is opened and your TrustID verification is initiated automatically as soon as your payment clears — no waiting for someone to action it.
  • You'll receive an email with a secure guest link and instructions to complete your online ID check — sent automatically, usually within a few minutes of your payment being received.
  • Open the link on your phone, photograph your ID and take a quick selfie — about 10 minutes. It expires after 14 days.
  • We then review the result and submit your verification to Companies House, usually within a further 24–48 hours. Companies House will email your personal code directly to you.

Privacy Policy

This policy explains what personal data Tax And Accounting Hub Ltd ("we", "us") collects when you use Director Personal Code, why we collect it, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and your rights.

Last updated: January 2026

1. Data controller

The data controller is Tax And Accounting Hub Ltd, Company No. 08408126, registered office 11 Holbeach Avenue, Shortstown, Bedford, MK42 0EG. For data protection queries, contact Kader Ameen, Director, at Kader@taxandaccountinghub.com.

2. What we collect

To carry out your identity verification, we collect: full legal name, former names, date of birth, nationality, country of residence, home address and 12-month address history, personal email address, mobile number, company name/number and role (director/PSC/LLP member/general partner/person filing), identity document details (type, reference number, expiry, country of issue), and — where the digital verification route is used — biometric data (facial image and liveness data) processed by our sub-processor TrustID.

3. Why we collect it (lawful basis)

  • Performance of a legal obligation — the Economic Crime and Corporate Transparency Act 2023 and associated Companies House identity verification rules require us, as an Authorised Corporate Service Provider, to verify your identity before submitting a verification statement on your behalf.
  • Substantial public interest (Data Protection Act 2018, Schedule 1) — for the limited processing of biometric data used for identity matching and fraud prevention.
  • Legal obligation and legitimate interest — for anti-money-laundering customer due diligence, which is a separate but related requirement to Companies House identity verification.

4. Who we share it with

  • TrustID, our identity verification sub-processor, for applicants using the digital (IDVT) route — TrustID processes document and biometric data solely to perform the identity check.
  • Companies House, to whom we submit an Identity Verification Statement containing your name, date of birth, home address, email address, and document type/reference/expiry/country of issue only — we do not send Companies House copies of your documents.
  • We do not sell or share your data with any other third party, and do not use it for marketing without your separate consent.

5. Retention

We retain all records relating to your engagement, including documents, review checklists, and records of any failed verification attempt, for 7 years from the date your engagement is completed (or, for a failed attempt, from the date of that attempt), in line with our regulatory obligations. After this period, records are securely destroyed. This period cannot be shortened at your request.

6. Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to access, correct, or (in limited circumstances, given our legal retention obligations) request erasure of your personal data, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to data portability where applicable. To exercise these rights, contact Kader@taxandaccountinghub.com. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk) if you believe your data has been mishandled.

7. Security

Data submitted through this website is transmitted over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. Access to your records is restricted to authorised staff of Tax And Accounting Hub Ltd and our identity verification sub-processor for the purposes described above.

8. Cookies

This site uses only essential cookies required for the site and application form to function correctly. We do not use tracking or advertising cookies. Our cookie banner lets you see what's in use. When you dismiss it we record that single preference in your browser's local storage (or, if your browser blocks local storage, in one strictly necessary first-party cookie named dpc_cookie_ack) so the notice does not reappear as you move between pages. That preference is kept for 12 months, contains no personal data or identifier, and is never shared. You can remove it at any time by clearing your browser's site data. No other cookies are set without your action.

9. Companies House public register note

Once your personal verification code is connected to your Companies House record, the public register will display your name, the name of your ACSP (Tax And Accounting Hub Ltd), our AML supervisory body (AAT), and the date your identity was verified. It will not display your documents, biometric data, or home address.

10. ICO registration

Tax And Accounting Hub Ltd is registered with the Information Commissioner's Office as a data controller.
